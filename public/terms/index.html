The ground rules for using this gateway — plain language, no traps.

The service

AI Gateway API is a self-hosted, open-source (MIT) REST gateway that routes chat requests to AI providers. You deploy your own copy on your own Cloudflare account; these terms describe fair use of the public demo instance at aigatewaybd.pages.dev and the code itself.

Fair use

Rate limits are enforced per IP to keep the demo alive for everyone. Do not use the service for illegal content, malware generation, harassment, spam, or to violate any upstream provider’s acceptable-use policy. Automated abuse may be blocked without notice.

Upstream providers

Replies are generated by third-party AI providers. Their own terms govern what you do with generated content. The gateway is a router: availability, accuracy and content of replies are the responsibility of the upstream provider that produced them, and the automatic failover system decides which provider answers.

The service is provided “as is”, without warranty of any kind. Free-tier upstream models may be slow, rate-limited or unavailable — that is exactly what the failover system works around, but no specific availability is guaranteed.
